Split Rush

Run · Split · Merge

Race down a glowing neon track where every gate asks one question: how many balls are you? Swipe to split your ball into two or four, swipe back to merge them into one, tap to jump. Tunnels only let the right formation through, walls only break under a single heavy ball.

  • Simple one-finger controls, hard to master
  • Speed boosters, PERFECT switches and OVERDRIVE
  • Daily Run — a new track every day, race your own ghost
  • Plays offline, no account needed, 11 languages
